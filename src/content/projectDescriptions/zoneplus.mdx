For <a href="https://hack.tum.de/past-events/events-2025/" target="_blanK">HackaTUM 2025</a>, I teamed up with <a href="https://devpost.com/DavidAtanasoski" target="_blank">David Atanasoski</a>, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/vinicius-agreste/" target="_blank">Vinicius Agreste Martines dos Santos</a> and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/merve-%C3%B6-968626259/" target="_blank">Merve Öztekin</a> to participate on the Logitech track and build a wellness plugin for _Logitech Actions+ ecosystem_. The plugin featured interactions with _webcams_, _haptic mouses_ and the _Options+ input panel_. We included multiple features, such as:
- Social media time spent trackers.
- Notion tasks management.
- Energy level and stretch reminders.
- Pomodoro timer.
- Drowsiness detection.

Although we did not win any prize for this, we were among the _shortlisted_ teams on the track, and we had a lot of fun creating this over the weekend!
